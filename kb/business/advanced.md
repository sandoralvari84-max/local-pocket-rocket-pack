# Business & strategy — advanced / academic layer (mentor-curated, round 2)

Builds on business-fundamentals.md. General education, not legal/tax/investment advice.

## Microeconomics
- Price elasticity of demand/supply; elastic vs inelastic goods; cross- and income elasticity.
- Marginal analysis: produce where marginal revenue = marginal cost; sunk costs are irrelevant to forward decisions.
- Market structures: perfect competition, monopolistic competition, oligopoly, monopoly — differ in pricing power and entry barriers.
- Game theory: Nash equilibrium, prisoner's dilemma, signalling; externalities and public goods.

## Macro context
- GDP, inflation (CPI), unemployment, interest rates; monetary (central bank) vs fiscal (government) policy; the business cycle.

## Corporate finance
- Time value of money: present/future value, discounting. NPV (invest if > 0), IRR, payback.
- Cost of capital: WACC; valuation by discounted cash flow (DCF) and by multiples (P/E, EV/EBITDA).
- Capital structure (debt vs equity), leverage and its risk.

## Accounting (depth)
- Accrual vs cash basis; the three statements link (net income → equity; cash flow reconciles).
- Ratios: liquidity (current, quick), profitability (gross/net margin, ROE, ROA), leverage (debt/equity), efficiency (inventory turnover).

## Strategy
- Porter: five forces, value chain, generic strategies; competitive advantage and moats (network effects, switching costs, scale, brand, IP).
- Frameworks: SWOT, PESTEL, BCG growth-share matrix, Ansoff matrix, Blue Ocean (value innovation), disruptive innovation (Christensen).
- Resource-based view: sustainable advantage from valuable, rare, inimitable, organised resources (VRIO).

## Operations & org
- Lean/Six Sigma, theory of constraints, unit economics at scale; organisational design and incentives.
