# Business — fundamentals (mentor-curated seed, round 1)

General educational overview; not legal, tax or investment advice.

## Economics basics
- Supply and demand set price; scarcity forces trade-offs; opportunity cost = the next best alternative given up.
- Marginal thinking: decide by the cost/benefit of one more unit.
- Fixed vs variable costs; economies of scale; break-even = fixed costs ÷ (price − variable cost).

## Startup & business models
- A business model = how you create, deliver and capture value (see the Business Model Canvas).
- Common models: subscription (SaaS), marketplace, transactional, freemium, advertising, licensing.
- Lean startup: build → measure → learn; test a minimum viable product (MVP) against real demand fast.
- Product–market fit: strong, repeatable demand from a defined segment, before scaling spend.

## Financial planning
- Three statements: income statement (profit/loss), balance sheet (assets/liabilities/equity), cash-flow statement.
- Profit ≠ cash; many profitable companies fail from running out of cash. Watch runway and burn rate.
- Unit economics: contribution margin per unit; CAC and LTV; aim LTV:CAC > 3:1 and payback under ~12 months.

## Go-to-market
- Know the customer and the problem; size the market (TAM/SAM/SOM); pick a focused beachhead.
- Pricing follows value and willingness to pay, not just cost-plus.

## Legal basics (very general — consult a professional)
- Choose a structure (sole trader, partnership, limited company) for liability and tax reasons.
- Put key agreements in writing; protect IP; keep business and personal finances separate; meet tax and filing duties.
