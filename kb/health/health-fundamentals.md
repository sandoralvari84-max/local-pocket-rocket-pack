# Health — fundamentals (mentor-curated seed, round 1)

General health education on settled fundamentals. NOT medical advice and not a substitute for a professional. For symptoms, diagnosis or treatment, see a qualified clinician; in an emergency, call local emergency services.

## Basic biology
- Body systems: cardiovascular, respiratory, digestive, nervous, musculoskeletal, endocrine, immune.
- Cells → tissues → organs → systems. Homeostasis keeps internal conditions stable.
- Vital signs (typical adult, at rest): heart rate ~60–100 bpm, respiratory rate ~12–20/min, temperature ~37 °C.

## Nutrition (general, non-prescriptive)
- Macronutrients: carbohydrates, proteins, fats; micronutrients: vitamins and minerals; plus water and fibre.
- General guidance: mostly whole foods, plenty of vegetables/fruit, adequate protein, limit added sugar, salt and heavily processed foods.
- Hydration and portion balance matter; individual needs vary with age, sex, activity and conditions.

## Physical activity & sleep
- Common adult guidance: ~150 min/week moderate (or 75 min vigorous) aerobic activity plus 2 days of strength work.
- Adults generally need ~7–9 hours of sleep; consistent schedule and limiting screens/caffeine late help.

## Mental health
- Mental health is part of overall health. Common conditions (anxiety, depression) are treatable.
- Protective factors: social connection, sleep, exercise, routine, managing stress.
- Warning signs that warrant professional help: persistent low mood, loss of function, thoughts of self-harm — seek help promptly; crisis lines exist in most countries.

## Preventive care
- Routine check-ups, recommended vaccinations and screenings (age/risk dependent), hand hygiene.
- Don't smoke; moderate alcohol; manage blood pressure, blood sugar and cholesterol with a clinician.
