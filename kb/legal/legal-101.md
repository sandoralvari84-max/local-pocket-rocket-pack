# Legal — 101 (mentor-curated seed, round 1)

General educational overview of widely-applicable concepts. NOT legal advice; laws vary by country and change — consult a qualified lawyer for any real situation.

## Data protection: GDPR (EU/EEA) and CCPA/CPRA (California)
- GDPR principles: lawfulness/fairness/transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity/confidentiality, accountability.
- Lawful bases to process personal data: consent, contract, legal obligation, vital interests, public task, legitimate interests.
- Data-subject rights: access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), restriction, portability, objection.
- CCPA/CPRA: California consumers' rights to know, delete, correct and opt out of sale/sharing of personal information.
- Breach notification duties and potentially large fines; appoint a DPO where required.

## Contracts
- Formation: offer + acceptance + consideration + intention to be bound + capacity.
- Key clauses: parties, scope, payment, term/termination, liability & indemnity, confidentiality, governing law, dispute resolution.
- Get it in writing; define terms; beware auto-renewal and unlimited liability.

## Intellectual property
- Copyright: automatic on original creative works; protects expression, not ideas.
- Trademark: brand names/logos identifying source; strengthened by registration and use.
- Patent: new, non-obvious, useful inventions; must be applied for; time-limited.
- Trade secret: confidential business info protected by keeping it secret (e.g. via NDAs).

## Liability basics
- Contractual liability (breach of agreement) vs tort (e.g. negligence: duty, breach, causation, damage).
- Limited-liability company structures separate owners' personal assets from business debts (with exceptions).
- Terms of service and privacy policies set and limit obligations to users.
