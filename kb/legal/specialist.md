# Law — specialist layer (precise facts, common myths) — NOT legal advice

General education. Laws vary by jurisdiction and change; verify locally and consult a qualified lawyer. Items marked
(US)/(EU)/(UK) are jurisdiction-specific.

## Copyright — the myths are dangerous
- Copyright is **automatic** on creation of an original work; no registration or © notice required. BUT (US) you must
  register before you can **sue** for infringement, and timely registration unlocks statutory damages.
- The **"30% change" rule is a myth** — there is no percentage that makes copying legal. Infringement turns on
  substantial similarity of protected expression.
- **"Poor man's copyright"** (mailing yourself a copy) gives essentially no legal protection.
- Ideas, facts, and methods aren't protected — only their **expression**. "It's on the internet" ≠ free to use.
- Fair use (US) is a **4-factor** balancing test (purpose, nature, amount, market effect) decided case-by-case — not a
  fixed word count. The EU/UK use narrower enumerated "fair dealing" exceptions. Duration is typically **life + 70 years**.

## Patents & trademarks
- Patents require novelty + inventive step + application; a **public disclosure can destroy novelty** (grace periods
  vary; the US gives a 1-year grace, much of the world none). The US is **first-to-file** since the 2013 AIA.
- Trademark rights arise from **use in commerce** (US common law) and are strengthened by registration; ™ = claimed,
  ® = registered. Distinctiveness runs fanciful → arbitrary → suggestive → descriptive → generic (generic can't be a mark).

## Contracts
- Formation (common law): offer + acceptance + **consideration** + intent + capacity. Civil-law systems don't require
  consideration. Verbal contracts are often valid but hard to prove; the **Statute of Frauds** requires certain
  contracts (land, >1 year, guarantees) to be in writing.
- An "agreement to agree" is usually unenforceable. Liquidated-damages clauses are enforceable only if a genuine
  pre-estimate, not a penalty.

## Data protection (EU GDPR)
- **Extraterritorial**: applies to processing of EU/EEA residents' data regardless of where the company is.
- Fines up to **€20M or 4% of global annual turnover**, whichever is higher. Six lawful bases; breach notification
  within **72 hours**; DPIAs for high-risk processing; a DPO where required.

## Liability myths
- An LLC/Ltd limits liability but the **corporate veil can be pierced** (fraud, commingling funds, undercapitalisation);
  directors have personal duties. Insurance ≠ immunity.
