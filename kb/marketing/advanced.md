# Marketing — advanced / academic layer (mentor-curated, round 2)

Builds on marketing-foundations.md. Theory, measurement and behavioural science.

## Brand & positioning theory
- Brand equity: Aaker (awareness, associations, perceived quality, loyalty) and Keller's CBBE pyramid (salience → performance/imagery → judgements/feelings → resonance).
- Positioning (Ries & Trout): own a distinct position in the mind relative to competitors; points of parity vs points of difference.
- Category entry points and mental/physical availability (Ehrenberg-Bass; Byron Sharp, "How Brands Grow"): growth comes largely from reaching light buyers and being easy to notice and buy.

## Behavioural science
- Cialdini's principles of influence: reciprocity, commitment/consistency, social proof, authority, liking, scarcity, unity.
- Framing, anchoring, loss aversion and default effects shape choices; use ethically.

## Pricing
- Value-based pricing (willingness to pay) beats cost-plus; price elasticity of demand measures sensitivity.
- Tactics: price metrics/tiers, bundling, decoy effect, psychological pricing; segment by willingness to pay.

## Analytics & measurement
- LTV = average revenue per customer × gross margin × average lifespan (or ARPA × margin ÷ churn).
- CAC payback period; LTV:CAC; cohort retention curves; marginal ROAS vs average ROAS.
- Attribution models: last-click, first-click, linear, time-decay, position-based, and data-driven; incrementality testing (geo/holdout) and Marketing Mix Modelling beat naive last-click.
- Experimentation: randomised A/B tests; watch statistical power, sample size and peeking; significance ≠ business importance.

## Channels, in depth
- SEO: search intent, topical authority, E-E-A-T (experience, expertise, authoritativeness, trust), technical crawlability/Core Web Vitals, internal linking.
- Lifecycle/retention: onboarding, engagement loops, winback; email deliverability (authentication, sender reputation).
- Growth loops vs funnels: outputs (new users/content) feed back into inputs.

## Strategy frameworks
- Porter's five forces and generic strategies (cost leadership, differentiation, focus); jobs-to-be-done; diffusion of innovations (Rogers), the chasm (Moore).
