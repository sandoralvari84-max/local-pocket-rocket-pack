# Marketing — worked examples (mentor-curated, round 3: reasoning by example)

Each example shows the method: restate → retrieve facts → reason in steps → check → concise answer. Imitate this shape.

## Example 1 — "Write a positioning statement for a budget-friendly meal-kit for busy parents."
- Restate: produce a positioning statement for a specific product and audience.
- Facts (marketing-foundations.md › STP, Positioning & value proposition): STP = segment, target, position; template = "For [target], who [need], our [product] provides [benefit], unlike [alternative]"; differentiation must be true, relevant, hard to copy.
- Steps:
  1. Target: time-poor parents who cook at home but find planning/shopping a chore.
  2. Need: quick, affordable weeknight dinners without planning.
  3. Benefit: pre-portioned, low-cost kits delivered weekly; ~20-minute meals.
  4. Alternative + differentiation: vs premium meal-kits (cheaper) and vs takeaway (healthier, cheaper per serving).
- Check: fills every slot of the template; the difference (price + speed) is true and relevant to the chosen segment.
- Answer: "For busy parents who want to cook at home but have no time to plan, [Brand] delivers affordable, pre-portioned kits for 20-minute weeknight dinners — unlike premium meal-kits that cost more and takeaways that cost more and are less healthy."

## Example 2 — "Which channels should a new B2B SaaS use for launch, and why?"
- Restate: choose launch channels for B2B SaaS and justify by funnel stage.
- Facts (foundations › Funnel/channels; advanced.md › SEO, growth loops): awareness → consideration → conversion → retention; owned/earned/paid; match content to stage; SEO rewards topical authority and intent; retention is the PMF signal.
- Steps:
  1. Awareness (B2B, considered purchase): content marketing + SEO on problem-intent keywords; LinkedIn; targeted paid search on high-intent terms.
  2. Consideration: case studies, comparison pages, a free trial/demo; email nurture.
  3. Conversion: trial→paid activation, clear pricing, sales touch for larger deals.
  4. Retention/expansion: onboarding, lifecycle email; measure cohort retention and LTV:CAC (> 3:1).
- Check: every stage has a channel; choices suit B2B's longer, research-led buying cycle; tied to metrics.
- Answer: Lead with SEO/content + LinkedIn for awareness, case studies + free trial for consideration, activation + pricing clarity for conversion, and onboarding/lifecycle email for retention — judged on cohort retention and LTV:CAC, not vanity reach.
