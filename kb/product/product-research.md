# Product research & management (mentor-curated seed, round 1)

## Discovery mindset
- Fall in love with the problem, not your solution. Validate demand before building.
- Continuous discovery: talk to customers weekly; pair discovery with delivery.
- Outcomes over outputs: measure the change in user/business behaviour, not features shipped.

## User interviews
- Recruit real target users; aim for ~5 per round to surface most issues, then iterate.
- Ask about past behaviour and specific recent events, not hypotheticals ("Tell me about the last time you…").
- Open questions, then stay silent; don't pitch or lead; separate what people say from what they do.
- Synthesise into patterns, jobs-to-be-done, and prioritised pains/needs.

## Competitive analysis
- Map direct, indirect and substitute competitors; compare positioning, features, pricing, and gaps.
- Find an angle rivals don't own; note their strengths honestly and where users are underserved.
- Watch switching costs and why users stay or leave.

## MVP strategy
- MVP = the smallest thing that tests the riskiest assumption and delivers real value.
- Prototype fidelity matches the question: paper/clickable for usability, concierge/"Wizard of Oz" for demand.
- Define a success metric and a kill criterion before you build.

## Prioritisation
- Frameworks: RICE (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort), MoSCoW, value-vs-effort matrix.
- Beware the build trap: shipping features nobody asked for. Tie each item to an outcome.

## Metrics
- Acquisition, activation, retention, referral, revenue (AARRR "pirate metrics").
- Retention is the clearest signal of product–market fit; a leaky bucket won't scale with more spend.
