# Business — fundamentals (mentor-curated seed, round 1)

General educational overview; not legal, tax or investment advice.

## Economics basics
- Supply and demand set price; scarcity forces trade-offs; opportunity cost = the next best alternative given up.
- Marginal thinking: decide by the cost/benefit of one more unit.
- Fixed vs variable costs; economies of scale; break-even = fixed costs ÷ (price − variable cost).

## Startup & business models
- A business model = how you create, deliver and capture value (see the Business Model Canvas).
- Common models: subscription (SaaS), marketplace, transactional, freemium, advertising, licensing.
- Lean startup: build → measure → learn; test a minimum viable product (MVP) against real demand fast.
- Product–market fit: strong, repeatable demand from a defined segment, before scaling spend.

## Financial planning
- Three statements: income statement (profit/loss), balance sheet (assets/liabilities/equity), cash-flow statement.
- Profit ≠ cash; many profitable companies fail from running out of cash. Watch runway and burn rate.
- Unit economics: contribution margin per unit; CAC and LTV; aim LTV:CAC > 3:1 and payback under ~12 months.

## Go-to-market
- Know the customer and the problem; size the market (TAM/SAM/SOM); pick a focused beachhead.
- Pricing follows value and willingness to pay, not just cost-plus.

## Legal basics (very general — consult a professional)
- Choose a structure (sole trader, partnership, limited company) for liability and tax reasons.
- Put key agreements in writing; protect IP; keep business and personal finances separate; meet tax and filing duties.

# Business & strategy — advanced / academic layer (mentor-curated, round 2)

Builds on business-fundamentals.md. General education, not legal/tax/investment advice.

## Microeconomics
- Price elasticity of demand/supply; elastic vs inelastic goods; cross- and income elasticity.
- Marginal analysis: produce where marginal revenue = marginal cost; sunk costs are irrelevant to forward decisions.
- Market structures: perfect competition, monopolistic competition, oligopoly, monopoly — differ in pricing power and entry barriers.
- Game theory: Nash equilibrium, prisoner's dilemma, signalling; externalities and public goods.

## Macro context
- GDP, inflation (CPI), unemployment, interest rates; monetary (central bank) vs fiscal (government) policy; the business cycle.

## Corporate finance
- Time value of money: present/future value, discounting. NPV (invest if > 0), IRR, payback.
- Cost of capital: WACC; valuation by discounted cash flow (DCF) and by multiples (P/E, EV/EBITDA).
- Capital structure (debt vs equity), leverage and its risk.

## Accounting (depth)
- Accrual vs cash basis; the three statements link (net income → equity; cash flow reconciles).
- Ratios: liquidity (current, quick), profitability (gross/net margin, ROE, ROA), leverage (debt/equity), efficiency (inventory turnover).

## Strategy
- Porter: five forces, value chain, generic strategies; competitive advantage and moats (network effects, switching costs, scale, brand, IP).
- Frameworks: SWOT, PESTEL, BCG growth-share matrix, Ansoff matrix, Blue Ocean (value innovation), disruptive innovation (Christensen).
- Resource-based view: sustainable advantage from valuable, rare, inimitable, organised resources (VRIO).

## Operations & org
- Lean/Six Sigma, theory of constraints, unit economics at scale; organisational design and incentives.
